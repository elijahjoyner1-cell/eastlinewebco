Is Your Contractor Website Ready for Phones?

Most people looking for a contractor are on a phone. Here is how to check your site in about ten minutes.

By EastLineWebCo · November 13, 2026

When a pipe leaks or a panel trips, people search on their phone and call the first business that looks trustworthy. If your website is hard to use on a small screen, that call goes to someone else. You do not need special tools to check yours. Open your site on your own phone and work through this list.

The 10-point phone check

  • Phone number is tappable. Tapping it should start a call.
  • Number is visible without scrolling. Put it at the top of the page.
  • Text is readable without zooming.
  • Buttons are easy to tap. They should be big enough for a thumb and not crowded together.
  • The page fits the screen. No sideways scrolling.
  • It loads quickly on cellular data. Heavy photos are the usual cause of slow pages.
  • Your services are obvious. A visitor should know what you do in a few seconds.
  • Service area is stated. People want to know you cover their town.
  • The contact form is short. Name, phone and a message is usually enough.
  • Proof of real work. Photos of finished jobs build trust.

Why the service area matters

Say the towns and counties you actually serve, in plain words, on your home page and contact page. It helps customers decide, and it helps search engines connect your business with local searches. Pair it with a complete Google Business Profile.

What to fix first

If you fail more than two or three items, start with the click-to-call button and page speed. Those change results fastest. A redesign is worth considering if the whole layout fights the phone screen; see redesign or new website?
