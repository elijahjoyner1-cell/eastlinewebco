Harbor & Pine Coffee Roasters: a website concept for a coffee roaster

A warm, friendly website concept for a fictional neighborhood coffee roaster, with weekly roast cards, a subscription club and a printed-style menu.

Harbor & Pine Coffee Roasters is a fictional business. This is a concept project EastLine Web Co designed to show our range. It is not a client engagement, and no client results are claimed.

The brief

A neighborhood roaster lives on regulars and walk-ins. The site has to feel like the shop: warm, personal and easy to scan on a phone while someone decides where to get their morning coffee.

What visitors need

Where and when can I visit, what is being roasted this week, and can I get it delivered regularly?

Design decisions

  • Warm rounded shapes and an oat, spruce, butter and tomato palette so the site feels like a corner cafe.
  • Weekly roast cards with tasting notes, so the product list is the content.
  • A subscription club section that explains the choice in plain steps.
  • A printed-style menu and a visit section with hours at the bottom, where people look for them.

What we built

Roast cards, a subscription club, a menu and hours, set in Fraunces and Karla. The concept is a single responsive page that works on phones, tablets and computers.

Why it suits a cafe or local retail business

A neighborhood roaster lives on regulars and walk-ins. The layout puts the answer to the visitor's first question in front of them early, and keeps the next step, such as calling, booking or browsing, close at hand.
