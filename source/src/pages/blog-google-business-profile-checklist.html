Google Business Profile Setup Checklist

Your free Google listing is often the first thing customers see. Here is how to set it up properly.

By EastLineWebCo · November 13, 2026

A Google Business Profile is the listing that appears in Google Search and Maps when someone looks for a business like yours. It is free, and for many small businesses it brings more visibility than their website does at first. Use this checklist when you set yours up.

Before you start

  • Decide on your exact business name, and use the same one everywhere.
  • Decide whether customers visit you or you travel to them. Service-area businesses can hide their home address.
  • Have your website address, phone number, hours and a few photos ready.

The setup checklist

  • Claim and verify. Google will ask you to confirm the business is yours.
  • Choose the right primary category. It is one of the strongest signals for what you appear for.
  • Add secondary categories that truly apply.
  • Fill in every field. Hours, phone, website, services and a short description.
  • List your services with clear names and short descriptions.
  • Set your service area to the cities or counties you really cover.
  • Add real photos. Your work, your team, your storefront or vehicle.
  • Turn on messaging or booking only if you will respond quickly.

After you are live

  • Ask happy customers for reviews and reply to each one politely.
  • Post an update or photo regularly.
  • Keep hours current, especially around holidays.
  • Check that your website address links to a page that loads fast on phones.

Common mistakes

Keyword-stuffing the business name, listing a service area you do not serve, and using stock photos are the three most common problems. Google can suspend profiles that break its rules, so keep everything accurate.
