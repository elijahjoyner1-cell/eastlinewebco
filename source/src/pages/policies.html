Project policies

A plain-language summary of how our projects work, so there are no surprises. Your written project agreement is the final word on any project.

Project timelines

Proposed timelines depend on the size of the site. They start after we receive your deposit and you have supplied the content, materials and access we need.

ProjectProposed timeline
Small website2 to 3 weeks
Standard website4 to 6 weeks
Larger or more complex website6 to 8+ weeks
Rush deliverySubject to availability

Rush projects

Rush delivery is not automatic. It is subject to our availability and needs an agreed, accelerated delivery schedule. A 20% rush charge applies to approved rush projects. It is added to the one-time project cost and does not apply to monthly maintenance fees.

What can slow a project down

Late content, late feedback or missing account access moves the schedule. Timelines are proposals, not guarantees.

Revisions and support after launch

Design revisions

Two design revision rounds are included in design and redesign projects. A revision is a change to the agreed design. It is not a completely new website direction or a request to rebuild the project from scratch. Additional revisions are quoted separately or billed at $50 per hour.

14 days of post-launch support

For 14 days after launch we correct defects in functionality that we agreed on and delivered as part of your project. This is not unlimited future maintenance, new feature development or ongoing content changes. For ongoing help, choose a maintenance plan.

Deposits and payments

Two payments

  • 50% deposit before work begins
  • The remaining 50% before final handoff or launch

What the deposit covers

The deposit is calculated against the agreed one-time project cost, including an approved rush charge. Monthly maintenance is billed separately and is never part of the deposit. The quote builder shows both totals.

Refunds and cancellations

Refunds and cancellations are handled according to your written project agreement, taking into account work already completed and the terms of that agreement.

Third-party charges

Domains, hosting, third-party subscriptions and payment processing accounts are bought and paid for by you directly. Those providers may have their own charges and subscription terms.

Ownership and access

Your accounts

You buy and pay directly for your own domain registration and website hosting. We can help you set up these accounts and deploy your website. You keep access to your domain, hosting and other relevant accounts, so you are never dependent on EastLine to keep ownership of or access to your website.

Your project files

Agreed project deliverables are transferred to you after full payment. This covers the custom-developed source code and designs we create for your project.

Third-party materials

These stay under their own licenses and terms, and using them in your project does not make you their owner: licensed fonts, stock images, software and plugins, third-party subscriptions, and external services and integrations.

Maintenance policies

  • Unused monthly time does not roll over
  • Additional work is $50 per hour
  • Emergency work is estimated separately
  • Monthly reports are in Growth and Priority only
