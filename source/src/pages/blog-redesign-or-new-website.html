Redesign or New Website? How to Decide

Both can fix an outdated site. Which one you need depends on what is actually broken.

By EastLineWebCo · November 13, 2026

If your website looks dated or is not bringing in enquiries, there are two paths: redesign what you have, or start over. The right choice depends on what is working and what is not.

A redesign usually makes sense when

  • Your pages and structure are sound but the look is dated.
  • The content is accurate and you want to keep it.
  • The site is on a platform you are happy with.
  • You want to keep the web address and any search visibility you have built.

A new website usually makes sense when

  • The structure no longer fits how your business works.
  • The site is built on something old that is hard to update.
  • You are changing what you offer, who you serve, or your brand.
  • Most pages need rewriting anyway.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Do customers find what they need on the current site?
  • Does it work well on a phone?
  • Can you update it yourself, or do you have to ask someone each time?
  • Do you own your domain and hosting accounts?
