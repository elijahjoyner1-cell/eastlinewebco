Fern & Thread: a website concept for a online boutique

A soft, product-first website concept for a fictional online boutique, with collection circles, a product grid with color options, a brand story and a newsletter signup.

Fern & Thread is a fictional business. This is a concept project EastLine Web Co designed to show our range. It is not a client engagement, and no client results are claimed.

The brief

A boutique store needs products to be easy to browse and a brand that feels personal. The design should flatter the products and make the next step obvious.

What visitors need

What is for sale, which colors and options exist, who is behind the brand, and how to hear about new arrivals.

Design decisions

  • A plum, blush, sage and clay palette that suits soft, natural products.
  • Collection circles for browsing by category at a glance.
  • A product grid showing color options up front.
  • A brand story section and a newsletter signup to build a returning audience.

What we built

Collections, a product grid, a story section and a newsletter signup, set in Cormorant Garamond and Jost. The concept is a single responsive page that works on phones, tablets and computers.

Why it suits a boutique or small online store

A boutique store needs products to be easy to browse and a brand that feels personal. The layout puts the answer to the visitor's first question in front of them early, and keeps the next step, such as calling, booking or browsing, close at hand.
