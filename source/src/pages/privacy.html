Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026. This policy explains what information EastLine Web Co collects through eastlinewebco.com and what we do with it.

Who we are

EastLine Web Co is a web design studio based in Fredericksburg, Virginia, run by Elijah Joyner. You can reach us at support@eastlinewebco.com.

Information we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us:

  • Contact and quote forms. Your name, email address, any phone number, business details, the services you are interested in and the message you write.
  • Email. If you email us, we keep the message and your address so we can reply.
  • Project materials. If you become a client, the content, files and account access you give us to build and maintain your website.

Our website does not have user accounts, does not sell anything online and does not use advertising or analytics trackers. Your light or dark theme choice may be remembered in your browser so the site looks the same on your next visit. That setting stays on your device.

How we use it

  • To reply to your questions and prepare quotes.
  • To plan, build, host and maintain websites for clients.
  • To keep our records and meet legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else sees it

Form submissions are delivered through a form-processing service (Formspree), and email is handled by our email provider. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads. These providers process data for us under their own privacy policies. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Social media

We have profiles on Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and LinkedIn. Those platforms collect their own data under their own policies. If you message or comment, we see what you send and may reply.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and client records for as long as the project and our business or legal needs require. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing support@eastlinewebco.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, and we will honor valid requests.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

We use reputable providers and take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@eastlinewebco.com. See also our Terms of Service.
