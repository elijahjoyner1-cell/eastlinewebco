Lumen Family Dental: a website concept for a dental office

A calm, trustworthy website concept for a fictional family dental office, with an appointment request card, plain-language services and an FAQ.

Lumen Family Dental is a fictional business. This is a concept project EastLine Web Co designed to show our range. It is not a client engagement, and no client results are claimed.

The brief

Many people are nervous about the dentist. The site should feel calm and clear, explain care without jargon, and make booking simple.

What visitors need

What services are offered, what a visit is like, and how to request an appointment.

Design decisions

  • Soft shapes and a navy, teal and mint palette to feel clean and reassuring.
  • An appointment request card near the top so booking never hides.
  • Plain-language service descriptions instead of clinical terms.
  • A what-to-expect section and an FAQ for first-time patients.

What we built

A booking card, services, what to expect and an FAQ, set in Manrope. The concept is a single responsive page that works on phones, tablets and computers.

Why it suits a dental or healthcare practice

Many people are nervous about the dentist. The layout puts the answer to the visitor's first question in front of them early, and keeps the next step, such as calling, booking or browsing, close at hand.
