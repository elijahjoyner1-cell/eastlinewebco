Swift White Glove Moving: a website for a boutique moving company

A nine-page marketing site for a Maryland-based white glove moving and premium delivery company that serves the DC, Maryland and Virginia (DMV) area.

The brief

Swift White Glove Moving handles high-value interiors: luxury furniture, specialty items and room-of-choice placement. The company is based in Maryland and serves the DMV area. It needed a website that feels as careful as the service, and that makes it easy for a private client to ask for a consultation.

What visitors need

Someone hiring a white glove mover is trusting a company with expensive, often irreplaceable things. They want to know what is handled, how the process works, where the company travels, and how to reach a real person. The site is organized around those four questions.

Design decisions

  • A restrained, premium look with generous spacing, so the site reads as boutique rather than bulk.
  • A four-step process (Consultation, Planning, Execution, Completion) so visitors know what to expect.
  • A service areas page with a coverage-check form for addresses on the edge of the service area.
  • A privacy-first testimonials page. Rather than publishing unverified quotes, it explains how the company handles client privacy. Verified client quotes can be added after launch.

What we built

Nine pages: home, about, services, service areas, testimonials, FAQ, contact, a thank-you page and a custom 404. The services page covers white glove delivery, specialty item handling, luxury furniture transport, room-of-choice placement, boutique moving support and private client service. The contact and coverage forms are set up to deliver enquiries by email. The client supplied the logo and the testimonial wording; EastLineWebCo designed and built everything else.

Why it suits a moving company

Moving is a local, trust-led decision. The site puts the service area, the process and a clear contact path in front of visitors early, which is what local searchers comparing movers look for.

Project status

The site is complete and the client is pre-launch. The client will own their own domain; once it is purchased, EastLineWebCo configures hosting and deployment.
