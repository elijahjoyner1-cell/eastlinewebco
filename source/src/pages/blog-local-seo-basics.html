Local SEO Basics for Small Businesses

Seven things that help a local business show up when nearby customers search.

By EastLineWebCo · November 13, 2026

Local SEO is the work of making your business appear when people nearby search for what you do. Here are the basics, in the order we would tackle them.

1. Claim your Google Business Profile

This is the foundation. Follow our setup checklist and keep it up to date.

2. Say where you work

State your city and the areas you serve on your website, in plain sentences. For example, a Fredericksburg business might name Fredericksburg, Stafford and Spotsylvania if it truly serves them. Only list places you really cover.

3. Give every service its own clear description

One page that explains each service in specific terms is more useful to visitors and to search engines than a vague list.

4. Keep your details consistent

Your business name, phone number and website should be identical on your site, your Google profile, social accounts and any directories.

5. Earn reviews

Ask satisfied customers for a Google review shortly after the job, and respond to every review.

6. Make the site fast and easy on phones

Most local searches happen on phones. Use our 10-point mobile check to see where you stand.

7. Be patient

Local rankings build over months, not days. New businesses and new websites need time to earn trust.
