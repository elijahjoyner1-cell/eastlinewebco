What the logo means

The sun is your business, coming into view. The three lines beneath it are the work that holds it up, and they get shorter on purpose: design is the longest because everything starts there, build is next, and support is the shortest line that never stops.

We kept it to a sun and three lines because that is how we like to build websites: nothing on the page that does not have a job.

What we believe

One page, one job

A page that tries to do five things does none of them. We decide what each page should make a visitor do, whether that is call, book or buy, then build only for that.

Speed is part of design

A visitor on a phone gives a page a few seconds. We compress images, keep code lean and test on real devices so your site is ready when they land.

You should be able to leave

Your domain, hosting and ad accounts are opened in your name. If you ever want to work with someone else, you keep everything and we hand over what we built.

Reports should be readable

Our monthly summary answers three questions: how many people came, what they did, and what we recommend next. No dashboards full of numbers nobody uses.
