02Who you will be talking to

I like building things, and I like helping people build theirs

I have always enjoyed technology and making things. I am naturally curious about electronics, software and hardware, and about figuring out how things work. Building websites lets me combine that technical side with creativity.

What makes the work meaningful is using those skills to help other people. I enjoy seeing a business owner with an idea, a goal or something they are working toward, and then helping bring that vision to life online.

I also understand that running a business takes real time and effort. A website should not become one more thing that makes life more complicated. It should help customers understand what you offer, find you, and take the next step toward working with you.

My goal with EastLine Web Co is simple: thoughtful, useful websites, and an online presence you can be proud of.

Why it is called EastLine

The sun is your business, coming into view. The three lines beneath it are the work that holds it up, and they get shorter on purpose: design is the longest because everything starts there, build is next, and support is the shortest line that never stops.

We kept it to a sun and three lines because that is how I like to build websites: nothing on the page that does not have a job.
