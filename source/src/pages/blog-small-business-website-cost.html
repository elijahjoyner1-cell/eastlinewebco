What a Small Business Website Costs

A plain-language look at what you are actually paying for, and how to compare quotes without getting lost.

By EastLineWebCo · November 13, 2026

Website prices can range from a few hundred dollars to tens of thousands, which is why the question “how much does a website cost?” is so hard to answer. The honest answer is that it depends on a short list of things. Once you know them, you can read any quote with confidence.

What drives the price

  • Number of pages. Each page needs its own layout, writing, images and checking on phones, tablets and computers.
  • Custom design or a template. A layout designed around your business takes more work than a stock one.
  • Functionality. Contact forms are simple. Online payments, booking systems and member logins are separate, larger jobs.
  • Content. If you supply the text and photos, the project is cheaper than if someone writes and shoots everything.
  • Ongoing care. Hosting, updates and small changes after launch are a recurring cost, not a one-time one.

Costs people forget

  • Domain and hosting. You should own these accounts yourself. They are billed by the domain registrar and host, not by the designer.
  • Copywriting and photography. Often priced separately.
  • Maintenance. Our monthly plans are $49, $99 and $179 and include 30 minutes, 2 hours and 5 hours of work respectively.

How to compare quotes

Ask every provider the same five questions: what is included per page, how many design revisions you get, who owns the domain and hosting, what happens after launch, and what costs extra. A low number that leaves these unanswered is rarely the cheapest option in the end.
