NO ETA: a website for a new streetwear brand

A brand site for a streetwear label that has not produced its first garments yet, with custom-rendered concept visuals in place of product photos.

The brief

NO ETA is a new streetwear brand from the same client as Swift White Glove Moving. The label has not made physical clothing yet, but the client wanted a credible brand site ready to collect interest ahead of the first drop. The client supplied the name; EastLineWebCo designed and built everything else.

What visitors need

A new clothing brand has to look like a real label before it has real products. Visitors want to see the style, understand what is coming, and have a way to hear about the first release.

Design decisions

  • Custom garment visuals, rendered by us, showing the planned pieces with realistic fabric and lighting.
  • Clear concept labeling. A banner and CONCEPT badges tell visitors the clothing shown is a concept render, not an available product.
  • "Get updates" calls to action instead of a store checkout, because nothing is for sale yet.
  • A dark, minimal layout that lets the garments lead.

What we built

A single-page React site with a product grid, concept notices, mobile-first layout and update sign-up buttons. The garment images were created from scratch for this project, including a boxy heavyweight hoodie with a scooped neckline.

Why it suits a clothing brand

Pre-launch brands live on anticipation. Honest concept labeling protects the client from implying products exist, while the visuals still show the direction of the label.

Project status

The site is complete and the client is pre-launch. The client will own their own domain; once it is purchased, EastLineWebCo configures hosting and deployment. A store can be added when products exist. [CONFIRM: launch date]
