Voltline Electric: a website concept for a electrician

A bold website concept for a fictional electrician, built to get the phone ringing with an emergency call bar, a clear service grid and a three-step process.

Voltline Electric is a fictional business. This is a concept project EastLine Web Co designed to show our range. It is not a client engagement, and no client results are claimed.

The brief

People who need an electrician are often stressed and on a phone. The site needs to answer one question fast: can you help me, and how do I reach you now?

What visitors need

A phone number they can tap, the services offered, the area covered and what happens after they call.

Design decisions

  • A persistent emergency call bar so the phone number is always one tap away.
  • High-contrast coal and volt-yellow colors with angled shapes that read well outdoors on a phone.
  • A service grid with short, specific descriptions.
  • A three-step process (call, visit, fixed) so visitors know what to expect, plus a service area section.

What we built

A call bar, a service grid, a process section and a service area, set in Barlow Condensed and Barlow. The concept is a single responsive page that works on phones, tablets and computers.

Why it suits a electrician or other trades business

People who need an electrician are often stressed and on a phone. The layout puts the answer to the visitor's first question in front of them early, and keeps the next step, such as calling, booking or browsing, close at hand.
