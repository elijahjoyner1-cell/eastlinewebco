Common questions

Do you accept my insurance?

We work with most major plans and will check your coverage before your visit. We also offer simple monthly plans.

Can I bring my child for a first visit?

Yes. We recommend a first visit around age one and keep it short, playful and pressure free.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call us. We keep time open each day for pain, broken teeth and lost fillings.
